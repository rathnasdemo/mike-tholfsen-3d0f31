Every publication starts with a point of view. This one starts with a simple
promise: the words, structure, and history remain yours.

## Begin with the question

Before choosing a cadence or polishing a logo, write down the question you want
to keep exploring. A durable question gives readers a reason to return and gives
you somewhere honest to begin when the blank page feels too large.

> A publication becomes distinctive through accumulated attention, not a
> single perfect launch.

For the first month, keep the practice deliberately small:

1. Publish one useful observation.
2. Explain the evidence behind it.
3. Invite a thoughtful response.

## Make the place your own

You can write in the Galascribe app, or edit the files in this repository
directly and commit them with git — both work, because everything Galascribe
needs lives in plain files.

Change the publication details, replace the author profile, and rewrite this
article in your own voice. The Editorial theme will use the same content across
its home, article, archive, series, and author views without locking it into a
page-builder format.
